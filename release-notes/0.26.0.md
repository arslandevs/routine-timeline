Your day, live.

- **Status follows the clock.** At a task's start time, *Not started* becomes *In progress*; at its end, it becomes *Done*. Only the moments that pass are applied, so manual changes are kept, custom statuses are left alone, and opening Obsidian catches up on what happened while it was closed. Per-device switch: **Settings → Status follows the clock** (on by default).
- **The current task pops, in every view.** Accent ring and glow, a *Now · 25m left* pill with a live dot, and a fill showing progress through the task. Timeline bars grow to full row height; Table rows get a tinted background and accent rail; Board cards lift with a progress line; Calendar blocks fill top to bottom. When the task ends it returns to normal and the next one takes over, updated every minute without a re-render.
