Timeline fixes.

- **Drag yesterday's and tomorrow's tasks without changing day.** The Timeline shows the day before and after the selected one; those bars were click-to-edit only. They now drag, resize, tick and track time just like the selected day's bars, each for its own date. A late task from yesterday (for example 20:00, or one running past midnight) can be moved straight from today, including across midnight into today.
- **New play/stop button on Timeline bars.** A round, high-contrast button pinned to the bar's left, shown on hover, while selected, or while the timer runs. The title slides right so it stays readable. Bars too short to hold it (5–10 minute tasks) get the button just outside their left edge, so it is always reachable and never blocks dragging.
